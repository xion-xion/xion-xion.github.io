About Me

I am a Multi-Disciplinary System Integration Engineer and IIoT Specialist dedicated to bridging the gap between the physical and digital worlds. My career took flight in the aerospace sector and autonomous drone R&D, which sparked my passion for engineering cohesive systems—from physical sensors and custom hardware up to cloud platforms and real-time enterprise dashboards.

I focus on making machines "talk and think." Rather than just writing software or wiring sensors, I architect end-to-end systems that automate manual processes, eliminate operational inefficiencies, and deliver measurable business value. I live for the moment when industrial data is digitized, allowing stakeholders to optimize their systems in real-time.

Key areas of proven impact include:

  • Enterprise IIoT Systems: Architecting centralized control loops, sensor telemetry, and Niagara framework supervisor dashboards that automate factory operations and optimize HVAC energy consumption.
  • Hardware R&D: Designing solar-powered floating nodes with C++ sensor-drift compensation, custom multispectral camera payloads, and custom PCB instrumentation at a fraction of standard commercial costs.
  • Entrepreneurial Leadership: Co-founding and managing a self-service laundry venture, establishing lean operational models and student-led repair SOPs that achieved full loan repayment in three years.

I am always seeking the next complex automation challenge. Whether you need to digitize a legacy manufacturing line, implement predictive maintenance systems, or develop commercial IoT devices, let's connect to engineer results.
