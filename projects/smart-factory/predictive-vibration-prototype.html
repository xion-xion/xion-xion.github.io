Predictive Vibration Monitoring Prototype (Phase 1)

Deploying sensor telemetry on a high-risk pusher to isolate faults and prevent stack collapses.

Role Solo Systems Integration Engineer
Timeline August 2025 - September 2025
Technologies Modbus RTU Vibration Sensors, EasyIO Gateway, BACnet, Niagara Workbench (Dashboards & Alarm Logic), SMS Gateway
Team & Vendors Executed end-to-end as a solo engineering project

The Problem & Context

The client's pneumatic firing machine pusher exhibited unpredictable, intense vibrations and jerking motions. This caused product stacks to topple inside the furnace, destroying up to 300 high-value pieces ($2 to $4 each) per incident. Troubleshooting was speculative, leaving maintenance passive and reactive.

The Action & Integration

I executed a solo prototype deployment, mounting a high-precision Modbus RTU vibration sensor directly onto the pusher mechanism. I routed the telemetry through an EasyIO gateway to the JACE, logged data to define baseline thresholds, and developed a Niagara dashboard. If the vibration exceeded baseline tolerances, the system triggered dashboard alarms and SMS alerts.

Measurable Business ROI

Empowered operators to intervene immediately before catastrophic in-furnace product crashes occurred, saving $300 to $1,200 in inventory per caught anomaly. The telemetry successfully isolated fault origins (proving whether the pusher or conveyor was at fault), moving the client to a predictive maintenance strategy.
