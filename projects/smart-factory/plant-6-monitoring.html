Temp & Status Expansion (Plant 6 Rollout)

Expanding telemetry to 287 controllers and introducing multi-port hub network isolation and automated alerts.

Role Lead Systems Integration Engineer
Timeline February 2026 - March 2026
Technologies Modbus RTU, RS-485 Multi-port Hubs, Python, SQL Database, Niagara Framework, WhatsApp API Integration
Team & Vendors Managed 1 hardware installation and wiring vendor

The Problem & Context

Plant 6 required manual recording of temperatures across 13 machines (287 heating zones), occupying 4.5 hours per shift. Additionally, system scaling exposed bottlenecks in the original Plant 5 design: Wi-Fi instability in the industrial environment dropped BACnet data, individual EasyIO controllers increased complexity, and single massive daisy-chains made diagnostic tracing very difficult.

The Action & Integration

I architected a rugged multi-port RS-485 network using physical hub isolation for each machine. This structure eliminated BACnet and wireless dependencies in favor of wired Modbus RTU routing to JACE. I developed a custom Python script that integrated with SQL to listen to Niagara alarms and automatically push alert messages directly to stakeholder WhatsApp groups. Upon successful deployment, I completely retrofitted the Plant 5 network with this hub topology.

Measurable Business ROI

Saved 4.5 hours of manual data collection per shift, enabling the client to reduce headcount requirements by 3 workers per day. System reliability rose significantly, and diagnostic times were cut by isolating network faults to individual machines. Stakeholders are now immediately alerted to temperature issues via automated WhatsApp messages.
