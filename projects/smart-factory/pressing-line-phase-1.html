Automated Pressing Line Data (Phase 1)

Digitizing 28 machine PLCs to automate reporting and unlock real-time OEE visibility.

Role Lead Systems Integration Engineer
Timeline October 2023 - November 2023
Technologies RS-485 Modbus RTU, JACE Controllers, PLCs, Niagara Workbench (Backend Logic & UI), Custom Java Scripting, SQL Database Integration
Team & Vendors Managed 1 wiring vendor, 1 Niagara software vendor, 1 PLC vendor, and 1 internal technician

The Problem & Context

Management completely lacked real-time visibility into pressing line productivity, machine states, and daily OEE targets. Relying on manual paper logs completed at the end of each shift introduced delays, reporting errors, and made it impossible to identify machine-stoppage root causes in a timely manner.

The Action & Integration

I integrated 28 PLCs across 14 pressing machines into JACE controllers using Modbus RTU. Integrated a SQL database with Niagara to map operator profiles to machines. Using Niagara Workbench, I wrote custom Java-based programs to calculate machine yield, target achievement, and operator productivity. I also designed a centralized dashboard with downtime-tracking analytics.

Measurable Business ROI

Automated 100% of data management and reports. Boosted operator productivity by 50%, enabling operators to complete 12-hour targets in just 8 hours (saving 80+ hours of labor per operator monthly). This allowed management to confidently increase daily production targets by 10% to meet demand. Machine downtime was reduced by 15%.
