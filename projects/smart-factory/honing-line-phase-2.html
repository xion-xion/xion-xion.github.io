Parallel Condition Monitoring (Honing Line - Phase 2)

Bypassing expensive legacy upgrades to install a parallel Modbus temperature and pressure network.

Role Lead Systems Integration Engineer
Timeline June 2024 - July 2024
Technologies Niagara Workbench (Dynamic UI & Alarm Logic), PT100 Temperature Sensors, Modbus RTU Pressure Sensors, Analog Input Controllers, SQL Database Integration, Piping Modifications
Team & Vendors Managed 1 wiring vendor, 1 piping installation vendor, 1 Niagara software vendor, and 1 internal technician

The Problem & Context

The honing line required precise temperature and pressure telemetry to monitor heater health, plan pump service, and prevent defects. However, a complete replacement of legacy machine controllers was bid as cost-prohibitive. Furthermore, different material types required specific pressure profiles, and setup errors caused high scrap rates.

The Action & Integration

I engineered a value-engineered parallel sensor network bypassing the legacy controllers entirely. I integrated PT100 temperature sensors and oversaw piping modifications to fit standalone Modbus pressure transmitters. In Niagara, I programmed logic to pull active material profiles from the SQL database and automatically compare them against live pressure sensor readings, triggering audible/visual alarms on setup mismatches.

Measurable Business ROI

Delivered the entire telemetry system at a 50% cost savings compared to replacing the controllers. The SQL-cross-referenced alarm system eliminated nearly 99% of pressure-based product scrap. Live trend graphs also allowed the maintenance team to transition from reactive repairs to predictive maintenance for pumps and heaters.
