Sensor Telemetry & Safety UI (Pressing Line - Phase 2)

Integrating smart telemetry and operator presence tracking to enforce safety protocols.

Role Lead Systems Integration Engineer
Timeline January 2024 - February 2024
Technologies RS-485 Modbus RTU, Smart Temperature & Pressure Sensors, PLC Data Extraction, Niagara Workbench UI & Logic
Team & Vendors Collaborated with 1 internal technician

The Problem & Context

The digital-only sensors on the pressing machines did not provide high-resolution numerical data, making quality optimization difficult. Additionally, management lacked a verifiable way to enforce new safety regulations that required operators to run pre-shift checks and remain physically present at their machines (acknowledging an HMI prompt every 10 minutes).

The Action & Integration

I sourced and retrofitted Modbus RS-485 capable temperature and pressure sensors, swapping physical wiring while keeping existing safety interlocks active. In Niagara, I mapped the new telemetry, updated the centralized dashboard with visual tolerance alarms, and designed a green/red visual indicator for safety checks. I also programmed a background validation module to log operator HMI response times.

Measurable Business ROI

Empowered production teams with granular temperature/pressure trends, enabling defects to be diagnosed instantly. Safety compliance and operator accountability were guaranteed via automated, unalterable reports of pre-shift checks and presence responses sent to management.
