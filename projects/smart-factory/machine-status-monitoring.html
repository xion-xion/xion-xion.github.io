Machine Status Monitoring Architecture (Phase 1)

Digitizing obsolete machinery status indicators via non-invasive hardware retrofits and alerts.

Role Lead Systems Integration Engineer
Timeline October 2023
Technologies Non-Invasive Relay Retrofitting, EasyIO Digital Input Controllers & BACnet Gateways, RS-485 Modbus RTU, JACE Controllers, Niagara Workbench, SMS Gateway
Team & Vendors Managed 1 wiring vendor; collaborated with 1 certified Tridium System Integrator

The Problem & Context

The plant lacked centralized visibility into machine operating states across 9 firing machines. Fault detection relied on physical control panel lights (auto, manual, emergency stop, etc.). Obsolete PLCs made software integration high-risk, so maintenance teams often took hours to notice faults, escalating minor issues into costly repairs.

The Action & Integration

I engineered a non-invasive, zero-downtime hardware retrofitting solution. I tapped directly into the physical panel lights (144 data points across 9 machines), terminating the signals into relays connected to EasyIO digital input controllers. Transmitted data via BACnet to JACE controllers. Using Niagara Workbench, I integrated this status data into the temperature dashboard and coded SMS alerts for critical faults.

Measurable Business ROI

Digitized legacy machines with zero downtime. Slashed maintenance response times from several hours to under 5 minutes. Real-time alerts and dashboard visibility empowered maintenance teams to react instantly, preventing minor stops from cascading into major delays.
