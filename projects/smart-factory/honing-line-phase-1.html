Honing Line Yield Tracking (Phase 1)

Designing custom proximity sensor mounts to expose undocumented product breakage.

Role Lead Systems Integration Engineer
Timeline November 2023 - December 2023
Technologies Niagara Workbench, RS-485 Modbus RTU, JACE Controllers, PLCs, Proximity Sensors, Digital Input Controllers, CAD/Hardware Design, SQL Database Integration, Custom Java Scripting
Team & Vendors Managed 1 wiring vendor, 1 fabrication vendor, 1 Niagara software vendor, 1 PLC vendor, and 1 internal technician

The Problem & Context

Management lacked real-time visibility into operator productivity and OEE across 8 honing machines. Furthermore, high-value parts (valued at $15 per piece) were breaking or being mishandled during the process. Because there was no input-versus-output tracking, identifying the exact rate or cause of product breakage was impossible.

The Action & Integration

I integrated 8 PLCs into JACE gateways. To uncover the breakage, I designed custom physical sensor holders in CAD, procured their fabrication, and drafted the electrical schematics. I directed a vendor to wire 16 proximity sensors at the machine outputs, terminating signals into Modbus digital input modules. In Niagara, I programmed logic to cross-reference SQL input counts against physical sensor outputs.

Measurable Business ROI

Achieved 100% data automation and identical OEE gains to the pressing line. Most importantly, the proximity sensors successfully exposed handling errors and machine faults, uncovering between $3,900 and $15,600 per month in undocumented product breakage, allowing management to immediately adjust handling procedures.
