Vibration Monitoring Scaling (Phase 2)

Expanding vibration telemetry to safeguard critical pumps and prevent plant-wide shutdowns.

Role Lead Systems Integration Engineer
Timeline April 2026 - May 2026
Technologies Modbus RTU Vibration Sensors, JACE Controllers, Niagara Workbench (Dedicated Trend Dashboards), WhatsApp API Integration
Team & Vendors Managed 1 wiring vendor; collaborated with 1 junior engineer

The Problem & Context

The client previously suffered a catastrophic structural failure when a malfunctioning exhaust fan pump bent its steel support frame. This incident took a critical firing machine completely offline for over 3 weeks. They needed to expand vibration monitoring to these high-risk pumps and the surrounding frame structure to prevent repeat losses.

The Action & Integration

I designed and deployed an expanded multi-sensor network. I wired 5 Modbus RTU vibration sensors (1 on the pusher, 3 on the pump body, and 1 directly on the steel frame) straight to the JACE controller to avoid gateways and increase speed. In Niagara, I created dedicated trend interfaces and integrated the automated WhatsApp API system to dispatch alerts immediately to maintenance staff.

Measurable Business ROI

Detected an exhaust fan pump operating significantly above baseline vibration limits, triggering a WhatsApp alarm. This enabled proactive maintenance before structural damage occurred, saving the client from repeating a catastrophic 3-week shutdown.
