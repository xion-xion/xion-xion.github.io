Intelligent AC System for Energy Reduction

Automating HVAC management with PLC and JACE controllers to eliminate runtime waste.

Role Lead Systems Integration Engineer
Timeline October 2024 - November 2024
Technologies Mitsubishi FX5 Series PLC, Niagara Framework, JACE Controllers, RS-485 Modbus RTU, HMI Programming, HVAC Control Logic
Team & Vendors Managed 1 PLC programming vendor and 1 electrical wiring vendor

The Problem & Context

Manual control of 11 factory AC units led to substantial energy waste. The designated operator turned on all units regardless of room occupancy or ambient temperature, and units were frequently left running in empty production zones due to human error. There was also no historical data to track wear-and-tear or system efficiencies.

The Action & Integration

I engineered an automated HVAC control system by integrating a Mitsubishi FX5 series PLC with Niagara. I modified the PLC program to output HMI data and guided the wiring vendor to install temperature/humidity sensors to JACE gateways via Modbus RTU. Niagara automatically switched the AC units on/off based on machine status, schedule profiles, and ambient sensors, while allowing safe manual override switches for maintenance.

Measurable Business ROI

Achieved a 10-15% reduction in plant-wide AC energy consumption by removing unnecessary runtimes. Additionally, AC maintenance costs fell by 10% through a predictive service schedule driven by JACE tracking of running hours and temperatures, preventing unexpected HVAC failure.
