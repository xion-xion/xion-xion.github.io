Custom Mechanical & Hardware R&D

To achieve high endurance, custom sensor payloads, and fully automated field operations, I focused heavily on custom electrical and mechanical fabrication. This moved the platform from basic drone assembly to a bespoke industrial system.

Custom Lithium-ion Battery Packs

Fabricated high-capacity Lithium-ion battery packs to replace standard off-the-shelf cells. Optimized the weight-to-energy ratio to extend continuous flight times to 45 minutes, enabling comprehensive plantation coverage.

Custom Multispectral Camera Payload

Designed and fabricated a custom casing holding three standard RGB Raspberry Pi cameras and one NoIR camera. Developed companion Python software on the companion Raspberry Pi to control synchronous frame capture across the array.

Physical Contact Charging Pads

Designed and integrated custom mechanical contact plates on the drone's landing gear to mate with corresponding spring-loaded charging pads on the solar-powered docking station, enabling automated outdoor recharging.

Precision Agriculture: Plantation Disease Monitoring

The Challenge:

Manual disease inspection across vast palm oil plantations was highly inefficient, labor-intensive, and slow, meaning disease outbreaks were often identified too late, resulting in significant crop loss.

The Solution:

Deployed the autonomous drone to perform automated, twice-daily aerial scanning flights. The multispectral camera payload captured RGB and NoIR imagery to monitor crop health parameters.

The Impact:

Replaced slow manual patrols with rapid, consistent aerial monitoring, delivering immediate disease-detection telemetry to plantation managers to prevent widespread crop loss.

Smart Aquaculture: Paddle Wheel Aerator Placement

The Challenge:

Incorrect placement of paddle wheel aerators leads to dead zones (low oxygen) and poor waste accumulation. If sludge does not collect at the pond's center drain, it raises toxic parameters and increases disease risk.

The Solution:

Used the drone to capture high-resolution aerial imagery mapping the exact positioning and orientations of paddle wheel aerators in shrimp ponds.

The Impact:

  • Disease Control: Verified that aerator-induced water currents effectively pushed sludge to the center drain, maintaining clean living zones.
  • Oxygen Optimization: Ensured aerators were aligned to distribute dissolved oxygen evenly across the entire pond, critical for shrimp survival and crop security.
