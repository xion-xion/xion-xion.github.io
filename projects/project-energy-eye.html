Hardware & PCB Prototyping

To deliver an affordable energy monitoring solution, I collaborated with a hardware partner who engineered a custom PCB designed to capture energy consumption without wire cutting or electrical shutoffs. The device utilizes a non-intrusive light sensor positioned over the utility meter’s flashing LED pulse indicator. Each flash corresponds to a fraction of a watt-hour consumed. By transmitting raw flash data over Sigfox and cellular e-SIM networks, the hardware achieves a completely wireless, zero-infrastructure connection that sends live metrics straight to the database.

Product Strategy & Lean Validation

The Energy Eye product lifecycle served as a critical lesson in product-market fit and Lean Startup methodologies. We originally targeted residential consumers (B2C), but direct field testing and customer interviews revealed a major roadblock: B2C users did not find energy visibility valuable enough to justify adopting the hardware. Identifying this early through hands-on deployments allowed us to stop over-engineering B2C features, saving significant R&D resources.

Using these field insights, I established a bilateral feedback loop to optimize the product strategy:

  • For Software: Translated field usage data into specific features for the software vendor, prioritizing hourly peak vs. off-peak consumption charts and cost projections that business owners needed.
  • For Hardware: Reported deployment issues (such as optical sensor alignment difficulties, casing clipping adjustments, and hard-to-reach device reset buttons) directly to the hardware partner to simplify the casing design and improve physical installation ergonomics.

This validation guided a successful strategic pivot to focus exclusively on B2B target sectors—specifically retail outlets, factories, and hotels—where energy consumption is a major operating expense and monitoring offers clear, high-yield cost savings.

Hands-On Deployment & Installation

As the deployment lead, I oversaw the end-to-end setup at customer locations. This involved conducting site assessments, ensuring stable signal coverage (Sigfox/Cellular), and conducting hands-on device testing. The clip-on casing allows the sensor to attach directly onto existing utility meters in minutes, ensuring a clean, zero-infrastructure rollout with no operations downtime.

React Native UI/UX & Data Analytics

I directed the customer journey and UI/UX design for the React Native mobile app and web portal. The core design philosophy was translating raw data points into clear financial terms. By showing peak vs. off-peak consumption ratios and tracking historical trends, the app empowers business owners to change operational behaviors (like running heavy machinery during off-peak times) to secure direct operational savings.
