Business Development: From Concept to Full Loan Repayment

Co-founded a self-service laundry facility under the corporate entity SK3 Kyodai Sdn Bhd to serve a local university campus. Directed the entire lifecycle from securing university tenancy contracts and vendor negotiations to designing the facility layout. Through strategic pricing and rigorous budget control, the venture reached profitability rapidly, enabling full repayment of the initial business setup loan within just three years.

Hands-On Mechanical Maintenance & 50% OPEX Reduction

Applied core engineering principles to lower operational overhead. Instead of contracting expensive external technicians for diagnostics and repairs, I took on the role of Lead Operations Engineer to personally troubleshoot mechanical and electrical issues on commercial washers and dryers. Implementing strict daily cleaning SOPs and bi-annual preventative maintenance schedules reduced operational expenditures (OPEX) by 50% and ensured an outstanding 99% machine uptime.

Operational Automation & Student Workforce Leadership

Streamlined the administrative workflow by implementing QuickBooks to automate financial tracking and reporting. Built a self-sufficient operational model by hiring and training on-site university students. This created a low-cost, highly responsive on-site team capable of resolving customer issues and maintaining the facility with minimal management oversight.
